Demo launcher. Open the three screens, then scan a table QR code with a phone that is on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.

1 Guest phone — scan a table

Each table has its own QR code; the table number is added to the order automatically.

2 Reception screen

Checks and approves new orders, edits them, prepares drinks & desserts, handles waiter/bill calls, closes bills.


Open reception

3 Kitchen screen

For a wall touchscreen. Shows approved food orders with notes; cooks tap dishes as they are ready.


Open kitchen

How to run the demo

  1. Open Reception and Kitchen (each asks for its PIN, then tap “Start screen” to turn on sounds).
  2. Scan table 1 with a phone (or click “open here”), add a few dishes and drinks with notes, and send the order.
  3. Reception hears a chime → Approve. Food appears in the kitchen; drinks and desserts stay at reception.
  4. On the phone, the guest watches each item change: waiting → being prepared → ready.
  5. Try changing the order at reception: items already marked Ready are locked; other changes flash on the kitchen screen.
  6. Use “Waiter” on the phone to call a waiter or ask for the bill, then close the table at reception.
